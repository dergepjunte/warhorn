![Warhorn](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/logo.png)

Warhorn is a wonderful mod which upgrades the boring Minecraft Raid mechanics.

![New Enemies](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/h_enemies.png)

![Sapper](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/sapper.jpg)

**Sapper.** Carries a powder keg up to your wall and lights it. Leaves a big hole.

<br></br>

![Battering Ram](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/ram.jpg)

**Battering Ram.** Four vindicators carrying a log. They keep hitting your wall until it breaks.

<br></br>

![Kidnapper](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/kidnapper.jpg)

**Kidnapper.** Puts a villager in a sack and runs for the forest. It's slower while carrying someone, so you can catch up.

<br></br>

![Catapult](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/catapult.jpg)

**Catapult.** Shoots burning pots into the village from the forest edge. Follow the smoke trail to find it.

<br></br>

![The Warlord](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/h_warlord.png)

![Grimvald the Ironhorn](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/warlord_face.jpg)

Grimvald the Ironhorn shows up after the third act.

- Three-hit axe combo
- Throws his axe, and it comes back
- Jumps and slams the ground
- Plants a war banner that buffs and heals his troops and calls in more of them
- Below 20% health he drops his armour, gets faster and hits harder

<br></br>

![Rebuild](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dergepjunte/warhorn/main/docs/images/h_rebuild.png)

Destroyed houses don't fix themselves. Hold logs or cobblestone and right-click a ruined house, and the villagers rebuild it while you watch. The HUD shows how much material each house needs.
